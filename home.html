Karate for Everyone

Karate is more than physical training. It encourages discipline, concentration, perseverance and respect for others.
At Kazi karate club, students work towards improving their karate techniques while developing qualities that can be applied both inside abd outside the club.

Who can join us ?

  • Anyone!!
  • To those who want learn a new skill, that takes dedication, discipline, respect and a lot more than giving 100%.
  • To people who are dedicated to learn martial arts, to defeat themselves, to rather be prepared and never get the chance to use it, than not be prepared and need to use the skill.

About the Club

Kazi Goju-Ryu Karate club started in the year 2001, exactly 25 years ago, (it being 2026 now). The club does authentick Okinawan Goju-Ryu karate. Training under the guidance of Nazeem Kazi.
