About the Club

Kazi Goju-Ryu Karate club started in the year 2001, exactly 25 years ago, (it being 2026 now). The club does authentick Okinawan Goju-Ryu karate. Training under the guidance of Nazeem Kazi.

The way of karateka

KARATE BEGINS & ENDS WITH RESPECT

1. Respect the Dojo
  • Bow when enter and leaving the dojo
  • Bow before stepping onto and off the training floor
  • Keep the dojo clean and treat it with care.
2. Respect your sensei
  • Listen attentively when the Sensei is speaking
  • Respond clearly with “Hai, Sensei!”
  • Avoid talking while instructions is being given.
  • Follow directions with humility and enthusiasam
3. respect training partners
  • Begin training with a bow and “Onegaishimasu”
  • End training with a bow and “Domo Arigatou”
  • Train to improve each other, never to prove yourself.
4. Personal Conduct
  • Arrive on time and ready to train
  • Remove jewellery and unnecessary accessories before class
  • Keep your phone on silent and avoid distractions.
5. Hygiene Matters
  • Arrive clean and well-groomed
  • Wash your hands and feet before training where possible
  • Keep your gi clean and in good condition.
  • Wear deodorant and...
6. A black Belt leads by example
  • Rank is not measured only by skill, but by character .
  • A true Karateka demonstrates:
  • • Respect
  • • Humility
  • • Discipline
7. Respect the dojo equipment
  • Dojo undo (training equipment) must be respected.
  • Be careful not to damage anything
  • wipe down before and after using equipment
  • Place equipment away carefully after use.

Respect

Our Mission

Kazi's Karate Club's mission statement is focused on the core ethical principles. It emphasizes more than just fighting; its goal is to develop a person's character as well as respect for the community.
The students and trainers of the dojo follow the mission statement which includes:

  • Pride in Heritage: The aim of the dojo is to be recognized as a proud follower of the authentic Okinawan Goju-Ryu.
  • Character Development: The students of the dojo train every day to be polite, modest, and honourable in their actions.
  • Mental Strength: Helping students build their courage and fighting spirit.
  • Discipline: Complying with high standards of discipline and behaviour as defined in martial arts.
  • Respect: Treating other karate practitioners with the utmost respect for their efforts.
  • Practical Application: Using martial arts discipline in everyday life.
